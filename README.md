# @webship-js/kanboard-steps

Cucumber step definitions for Kanboard, the open-source Kanban project management software, a pack for
[webship-js](https://github.com/webship/webship-js) (Playwright and Cucumber): logging in, a project and who may open
it, its board with its columns and swimlanes, one task, open or closed, its comments, tags, assignee and subtasks, a
new task, and what a viewer may not do.

The steps, their documentation and CI come with the first release, `2.0.0`.
